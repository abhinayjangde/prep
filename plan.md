## GATE 2028
### Phase 1 — Engineering Mathematics (Days 1–14)

The syllabus includes Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra, Calculus, and Probability & Statistics.

| Day | Topic | What to finish | Practice |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logic | Propositional logic | 15–20 problems |
| 2 | Logic | First-order logic | PYQs |
| 3 | Sets & Relations | Sets, relations | Problems |
| 4 | Functions | Functions, properties | PYQs |
| 5 | Partial Orders | Posets, lattices | PYQs |
| 6 | Algebraic Structures | Monoids, groups | PYQs |
| 7 | Graph Theory | Connectivity | Problems |
| 8 | Graph Theory | Matching, colouring | PYQs |
| 9 | Combinatorics | Counting | Problems |
| 10 | Recurrence | Recurrence relations | PYQs |
| 11 | Generating Functions | Generating functions + revision | PYQs |
| 12 | Linear Algebra | Matrices, determinants, linear equations | Problems |
| 13 | Linear Algebra | Eigenvalues/eigenvectors, LU | PYQs |
| 14 | Calculus + Probability | Limits, differentiation, integration + probability basics | Mixed test |

Important: Probability also contains random variables, distributions, mean/median/mode/standard deviation, conditional probability and Bayes theorem, so continue these during your later revision sessions.
